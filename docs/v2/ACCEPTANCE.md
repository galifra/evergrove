# Your acceptance walkthrough (P0.11)

About fifteen minutes. Do it on the preview address first, then on production. Each line says what you should see. Lines marked (about a cent) call the AI once.

1. **Open the address.** The tree appears with your data (after importing your backup on a preview). Nothing is missing.
2. **Open the app menu** (top left). You see every app and Jarvis. Tap **Money**: the address becomes `/money` and the Money screen opens. Refresh: it stays.
3. **Install Money** (browser menu, Install app). It opens in its own window with its own icon and colour.
4. **Old link.** Paste `.../#/app/tasks` in the address bar. It lands on `/tasks`.
5. **Log a workout in Body.** Open the tree in another tab: the tree has grown, with no reload.
6. **Open Jarvis** (`/jarvis`). He greets you by the time of day. If he does not know your name he asks once; answer, and the greeting now uses it. (Settings has a Your name box to change it.) He shows what is on today.
7. **Tell Jarvis "add a task to buy milk"** (about a cent). Open `/tasks` in another tab: the task is there.
8. **Type "remember that I run best in the morning".** He confirms. Open the memory screen (`/jarvis/memory`): the note is listed. Type "forget that": he asks first; say yes and it is gone. Add a note about a doctor or money: it shows as "Private note" until you tick Show private notes.
9. **Open the log** (`/log`). Search "milk": the event is there, with who wrote it. Money contents are hidden until you press Show private.
10. **Notes from Jarvis.** Under his greeting, if something needs you (an overdue bill, a stalled goal), he shows up to two notes, each with a way to act. Press the thumb up on one and "Not useful" on another: the second is gone, and Jarvis settings lists it under Muted for 30 days with an Unmute button.
11. **Goal coach.** If a goal has stalled, its note has Split it into steps. Press it: you see the tasks he would add. Add them and check `/tasks`; each is linked to the goal.
12. **Speaking up.** In Jarvis settings set "Jarvis speaks up" to Never. The notes disappear. Set it back to Only when necessary.
13. **Weekly review** (`/jarvis/weekly`) shows what grew, what stalled, wins, one suggestion and one question. "Put it in my words" writes it in his voice (about a cent, once a week at most).
14. **Ask his opinion.** In the chat type "what do you think about my week" (about a cent). Read it: is it honest, kind, short and about your real week? Press the thumbs on it; on a thumb down you can say what was off.
15. **AI budget** (Jarvis settings, and Evergrove settings): shows what this month has cost, what it went on, and where the 80% ration line is.
16. **Turn the sound on** in Jarvis. Ask "what's on today". He reads the answer aloud. Stop button works.
17. **Tonight at 9 PM** the notification arrives. Tap it: `/jarvis/brief` opens. On a Sunday it also says your weekly review is ready. Anything private is only named generally.

If anything looks wrong, note the number and what you saw. Nothing here needs technical knowledge.
